Our Story

One CH Steel.

CH Steel is the incorporated Culham + Herrick delivery company, registered on 24 December 2015 with 50% shareholding held by Culham Engineering and 50% by The Herrick Corporation. Clients work through one unified interface for planning, fabrication, coatings, logistics, and erection.

Partnership Story

Operating Together For Over A Decade.

Since 2015, Culham and Herrick have worked through CH Steel as one accountable delivery structure, combining local execution depth with international-scale steel experience.

Herrick Capabilities

International Scale Behind One Delivery Model.

High-Rise Expertise Airport + Civic Scale Power + Petrochemical Scale Seismic Delivery
  • 100+ years of Herrick heritage applied to complex steel programmes.
  • High-rise, airport, civic, power, and petrochemical delivery experience.
  • Model-driven engineering and shop drawing workflows using TEKLA and AutoCAD.
  • Certified quality and welding frameworks including ISO 9001:2015, AWS, AISC, CWB, LRQA, and SCA-aligned systems.

Culham Capabilities

Local Delivery Strength Across The Full Scope.

Local Execution Depth 65+ Years Delivery History Coatings + Fabrication Heavy Haulage + Crane
  • Established in 1958, with over 65 years of fabrication, construction, and project delivery experience.
  • Operations anchored by an 8.3ha Whangarei site with North Island support capacity.
  • Heavy fabrication, pressure vessels, tanks, marine, petrochemical, coatings, transport, and crane capability.
  • Direct harbour logistics access for efficient import, export, and transport of fabricated equipment.
  • Certified systems include ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, AS/NZS 5131, and ISO 3834-aligned controls.
